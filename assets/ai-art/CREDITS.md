# AI art inputs — provenance

Three images from the local 2024 AI-art archive (`E:/Multimedia Files/AI Art`),
resized to 1200 px and re-encoded at JPEG q86 for the demo.

- `demon-christ.jpg` — steampunk skull relief, source `Horror/Horror - Demon Christ/00397-4083925434.jpg`
- `postapoc-city.jpg` — isometric post-apocalyptic town, source `City/City - Postapocalyptic/00035-3001225182.jpg`
- `ruins.jpg` — expressionist ruins, source `Faves Showcase/Mix/00064-3499674084.jpg`

**Generation record:** AI-generated, 2024, local diffusion-era archive. Model,
seed, prompt and sampler were not recorded with the files. These are demo
inputs, not evidence of any specific pipeline.

They are used here to exercise PhotoCraft's batch/actions engine, and are
re-encoded only; the treatment is applied by `assets/photo/batch-actions.json`.
